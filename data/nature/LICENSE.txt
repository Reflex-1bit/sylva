Third-party data keeps its own licence
  The databases contain facts taken from other open sources. Those facts
  stay under the licence of their source, not under this one.
  - GBIF names and usage keys: CC BY 4.0 (https://www.gbif.org). Cite GBIF
    when you reuse them.
  - OpenAlex records (DOI, title, year, institutions, topics): CC0
    (https://openalex.org).
  - Crossref bibliographic metadata (DOI, title, year, licence flags):
    metadata is free to reuse (https://www.crossref.org/documentation/).
  - NACE Rev.2.1 / CNAE-2025 codes and the Horizon Europe TRL wording are
    public-body classifications and definitions, quoted for reference.
  Publisher abstracts are not stored. A supporting sentence is quoted only
  from papers whose own licence is CC BY or CC0.

Not covered, link only
  AskNature text and taxonomy are not copied. The database holds links to
  AskNature pages in the "see_also" field and nothing else from that site.
  Sylva's own data/species_db.json is a different dataset and is not covered
  by this notice.

Status of the licence choice
  CC BY 4.0 is the current choice. Whether to use CC BY 4.0 or CC0 is still
  open (connected-systems/07-building-the-nature-arm.md, section 7.9,
  item 4). Until that is settled, treat CC BY 4.0 as binding.

Warranty
  The data is provided as is. Records marked ai_extracted have not been
  checked by an expert. Read the caveat field on every record before using
  it in a decision.
